SURFACE · APPROVAL

Decisions waiting on your call

The system has reasoned. It now needs your YES/NO. Each item is a decision packet with a recommendation, the trade-offs, and the consequence if you ratify or defer.

Phase B · Building

Approval is the spine of the founder loop.

This surface is the only place where you authorize the system to act on your behalf. Everything else is reading or reviewing. Here, you ratify.

We're building this next because it is what makes the rest of Kilonova safe to scale — without a clear approval gate, every other surface becomes ambiguous.

What it will contain (v1)

  • Decision packets · one per pending choice, with recommendation + risk + rollback path.
  • SLA timers · each packet has a default action if you don't decide within the window.
  • Ratify / Defer / Send back · three primitive actions, audit-logged.
  • Inline comments · ask the council for a re-reasoning before deciding.
SURFACE · COMMITMENTS

What you and the system have promised

Everything you've said yes to. Everything pilot is doing on your behalf. Everything the council is owed. One ledger.

Phase C · Specced

A commitment is a promise with a deadline and a witness.

This surface unifies what you committed to in meetings, what pilot picked up from your decisions, and what the council is tracking against. Each commitment shows owner, due date, current state, and the audit trail.

Built after Approval — because most commitments are born in that surface.

What it will contain (v1)

  • Commitment ledger · grouped by owner (You · Pilot · Council · External).
  • Cadence view · this week · this month · this quarter.
  • Drift alerts · pilot flags when a commitment is at risk before you ask.
  • Backlinks to source · each commitment cites the brief, decision, or call that birthed it.
SURFACE · STATUS

Where everything stands, right now

The pulse view. What's healthy, what's drifting, what's blocked. No reading required — glance and move on.

Phase D · Specced

The cockpit glance.

Status is the only surface designed to be consumed in under 30 seconds. It shows the health of every active pilot engagement, every open commitment, every running council session, and every signal that's escalating.

Built last in the founder loop because it depends on the other three surfaces being live to draw signal from.

What it will contain (v1)

  • Health strip · pilots · commitments · approvals · signals — green/amber/red at a glance.
  • Active reasoning · what NICHOLAS and the council are running right now.
  • Drift map · which engagements are moving away from baseline.
  • Queue depth · what's about to land in Reading and Approval.
