You came in with a real trade-off, not a sequencing question. Both motions have signal in the substrate: the founder-OS waitlist is filling, and the Authority Avatar's LinkedIn anchor just hit signature engagement. You asked which one to put your weight behind in Q3.

The wrong answer would be: "do both, sequence them." That sounds reasonable but quietly fails: running both at half-power produces neither validation nor compounding. The substrate is clear on this: half-runs miss the moment.

The right answer turns out to depend on a question you didn't ask: which motion makes the OTHER one easier later?

01The two motions

What we're actually choosing between

Authority is the move where Kilonova publishes thought leadership at the rate the substrate can support. Drafts compound through the Authority Avatar, the soundbite library hardens, the territory of "AI-native company building" gets staked publicly. The funnel is indirect: founders who read the work qualify themselves into the waitlist.

Growth Campaign is the move where Kilonova runs a paid + outbound push for the founder-OS waitlist directly. The funnel is direct: ads → landing page → signups. The Avatars optimize copy variations. The metric is conversion rate and CAC.

Both motions can ship. The question is which one we put behind. The substrate suggests the answer isn't symmetric. Council deliberation · turn 7

04What this could miss

The risks worth naming

Risk 1 · Authority is slow. If runway tightens mid-Q3, you'll wish you had Growth Campaign already running. The mitigation: Growth Campaign is warm, not cold. The infra is built, the Avatar is configured, the landing page is live. Switching it on takes a week, not a month.

Risk 2 · Authority depends on you. Bruno's voice is the asset. The Authority Avatar can draft, but the calibration loop is your ratify rate. If your attention drops, the Avatar's drafts drift. Mitigation: this is what the Reviewer Avatar exists for. A quality gate before the founder queue, so you don't have to read every draft.

Risk 3 · Adjacent voices catch up. Lindy's "persistent agents" framing is in the same neighborhood. If they sharpen first, your distinction blurs. Mitigation: the Avatar/agent distinction is in the substrate as a load-bearing claim. Every Authority Avatar draft references it, hardening the position daily.
